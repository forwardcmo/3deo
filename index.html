The misconception

You probably think you know
what Geiger-mode lidar is.

Most of what the industry "knows" comes from early systems that simply fell short. Learn the rest of the story about a technology that's field-proven in military deployments. Our next generation systems are a completely different animal. Here's the honest comparison.

Conventional / linear-mode
  • Needs thousands of photons per return — limiting altitude and speed
  • Low flying, narrow swaths, repeated passes
  • Expensive to cover wide areas at useful density
  • Shadows under canopy and in urban canyons
3DEO Geiger-mode
  • Detects an object from just a few photons — fly higher, faster, wider
  • High resolution from 27,000+ ft at 30 cm GSD or less
  • Agile geo-referenced scanning captures 4–6 viewpoints in a single pass
  • Mitigated shadows in finished, high-density point clouds
