The problem

What’s at stake.

Forest and fire managers need accurate canopy and terrain data over enormous areas. Single-look lidar leaves voids under dense canopy, and low-altitude collection makes regional coverage slow and expensive.

What’s required

What the data has to do.

Wide-area, high-density data that penetrates foliage to recover both canopy structure and bare-earth terrain — refreshed often enough to be useful for planning.

How 3DEO helps

From scoping to answers.

Sees under the canopy

Multi-angle agile scanning fills the gaps a nadir look misses, recovering ground returns beneath dense vegetation.

Digital defoliation

Acadia separates canopy from terrain, producing clean bare-earth DTMs alongside canopy-height models.

Wildfire fuel at scale

High-density structure data across whole forests feeds fuel-load and fire-behavior models.
