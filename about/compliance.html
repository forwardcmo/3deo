Compliance

How we handle data, export, and quality.

3DEO works with both government and commercial clients, and we take the handling of sensitive data and technology seriously. This page is an overview — contact us for documentation specific to your engagement.

Data confidentiality

Point clouds and derived products are shared under a mutual confidentiality agreement. Access to the full data viewer requires agreeing to those terms.

Export-control awareness

We are mindful of U.S. export-control obligations that can apply to advanced sensing technology and data, and we work with clients to handle engagements appropriately.

Quality & accuracy

Deliverables are validated through our Acadia pipeline, with metadata for reflectivity, height above ground, and point confidence.

Responsible use

We partner with clients whose use of geospatial data is lawful and responsible, and we’re glad to discuss safeguards for sensitive collections.

Need specifics?

For confidentiality agreements, export-control questions, or documentation for procurement, contact sales@3deolidar.com.
