Software Suite

From raw photons to finished answers.

Acadia is 3DEO’s end-to-end software suite for processing raw Geiger-mode lidar into finished, high-density 3D point clouds — with the metadata your workflow needs.

Download brief (PDF) →Software & Systems (PDF)
The pipeline

Raw data → aggregated point cloud → answers.

01 · RAW DATA

Individual photon detections

Millions of single-photon returns per second — real signal mixed with significant noise.

02 · AGGREGATED

Complete picture of the scene

Detections are aggregated and denoised into a dense point cloud with minimal shadowing.

03 · ANSWERS

Labeled, analysis-ready

Points labeled with relative reflectivity, height above ground, and confidence.

Deliverables

High-resolution terrain and surface models.

  • High-resolution DTMs and DSMs
  • Points labeled with relative reflectivity & confidence
  • Typically 10–25 GB per minute of recording
  • Noise removed, shadows minimized
Integration

Extensible
